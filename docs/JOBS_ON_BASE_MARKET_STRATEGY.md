# JOBS on Base Market Strategy

Strategy: proof jobs create proof cards; proof cards create reputation; reputation routes better opportunities; better opportunities create more proof.

$JOBS is the native coordination asset for proof-based AI workflow work on Base. $JOBS is additive and does not replace the GoalOS product ladder. $JOBS coordinates proof-based work around GoalOS and must not be promoted as an investment.

Canonical flow: Sponsor posts a job using $JOBS. Builder claims the job with $JOBS. Builder submits proof with a $JOBS bond. Reviewer validates proof with a reviewer bond. Approved proof creates a proof card. Approved proof creates or upgrades a credential. Credential and proof history increase reputation. Reputation unlocks better jobs. Better jobs create more proof.
