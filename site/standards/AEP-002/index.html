AEP-002 — Evidence Docket Standard

AEP-002 defines the Evidence Docket: the portable proof package for machine work, including commitments, claims, evidence, ProofPackets, evals, risks, costs, policy decisions, Selection Certificates, rollout, rollback, and public-safe reports.

AEP-001 Constitution · Download PDF · Schemas · Templates · Examples

Canonical Law

No proof, no evolution.
No eval, no propagation.
No rollback, no release.

What AEP-002 Standardizes

Claims Matrix

Separates what is claimed, what evidence supports it, and what is not claimed.

ProofPackets

Atomic evidence units for traces, evals, tool calls, policy decisions, and outputs.

Selection Certificate

Records whether a capability may be promoted, canaried, rejected, revised, archived, or rolled back.

Public Boundary

AEP-002 proves accountability without leaking private intelligence. Public reports must preserve claim boundaries and keep sensitive prompts, data, traces, tools, and protected details private.
